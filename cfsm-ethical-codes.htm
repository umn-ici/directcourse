Ethical Codes

College of Supervision, Management, and Leadership (CFSM)

Ethical codes or codes of conduct are part of professional practice. You need to know about any codes that apply to your specific licenses or certifications. Your employer may also expect you to follow a specific code.

The National Alliance for Direct Support Professionals (NADSP) Code of Ethics (COE) is helpful for any professional who works directly with people. It provides helpful support for staying person-centered and supporting self-determination. Content in this training product aligns with this code of ethics. Read the full text of the NADSP COE.

You may find it helpful to refer to these or other codes when finishing reflection activities in the materials.
